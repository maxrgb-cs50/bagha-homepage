# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS (user decision), no build step. Source in `site/`.

## Users

Equally weighted, anywhere in Germany (served digitally) and locally in and around Regensburg:
- **Unternehmer** (small and mid-sized businesses, self-employed) needing Finanz- und Lohnbuchhaltung, Jahresabschluss, ongoing tax advice, succession.
- **Privatpersonen** needing Einkommensteuererklärung, rental income, Bescheidprüfung.
- **Existenzgründer** needing support from founding through the first years.

They arrive looking for a tax advisor they can trust and want to judge quickly whether this practice fits and how to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

The website of Steuerkanzlei Bagha, the practice of Steuerberater Human Bagha in Regensburg. Success means **contact requests and first consultations** (Erstgespräche).

## Positioning

**Digital and nationwide, yet personal.** The practice works fully digitally (DATEV Unternehmen online, DATEV Upload mobil for receipts by phone photo; conversations by video or phone) and takes clients from all of Germany, while welcoming in-person meetings in Regensburg. One permanent, personal contact person, the owner himself, instead of mass processing.

Copy rules: promise a "zügige" personal reply but no specific hour count; do not name the video tool or any signature/identification tool; describe mandate, power of attorney and legally required identification only generally.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Service catalogue: titles verbatim from `steuerkanzlei-bagha-design-system/leistungen.md`, in the order Unternehmer, Privatpersonen, Existenzgründer.
- Contact form has no backend; it composes a mailto to h.bagha@steuerkanzlei-bagha.de.
- Open / not yet provided: phone number, street address, office hours, Impressum and Datenschutz pages (placeholders marked `TODO` / `.placeholder` in `site/index.html`).

## Brand Commitments

- Design system in `steuerkanzlei-bagha-design-system/` is binding (palette, Cormorant Garamond + Hanken Grotesk, eyebrows, italic second lines, 2px/4px radii).
- German, formal "Sie". The owner speaks as **"ich"** throughout (user decision, overrides the design system's "wir").
- No emoji, no exclamation marks, no fear-mongering, never promise savings or refunds (Berufsrecht).
- Impressum + Datenschutz in every footer; consent checkbox on every form.
- Logo: HB monogram + "Steuerkanzlei Bagha" wordmark, vectorised in `site/assets/`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Owner profile and services: e-mail "Leistungen & über mich" (`Gmail - Leistungen & über mich.pdf`) and `leistungen.md`. Facts: in Steuerberatung since 2018, Steuerberater since 2024, B. Sc. FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg, M. Sc. BWL Universität Regensburg.
- Photos: `Foto 1.jpg` (portrait, only 233×300), `Foto 2.png` (consultation scene).
- No testimonials, client logos, reviews, case studies or figures exist. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Personal over institutional: every page should feel like talking to Human Bagha, not to a firm.
2. Clarity before persuasion: explain terms, state what happens next, never pressure.
3. Only true claims: no invented proof, promises or numbers.
4. The path to a first conversation is always one step away.
